Privacy Policy

App: Neon Drift: Space Run
Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Neon Drift: Space Run ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the game on Android. The App is developed by Gyanadipta Pahi ("Developer"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location. We do not collect personal information directly.

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Game progress, high scores, coins and diamonds
  • Game settings (sound, music, vibration, etc.)

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

2. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that collect information automatically in order to show ads and keep the App working. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) - https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services - https://policies.google.com/privacy

These services may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, app interaction data, and crash/diagnostic data. This is used to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve performance.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob, which may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your device settings and region. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), consent is requested before personalized ads are shown.

4. How We Use Information

  • To provide and run the game
  • To display advertisements
  • To analyze and improve app stability and performance
  • To comply with legal obligations

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with the third-party service providers listed above, solely for the purposes described in this policy, or when required by law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you delete it by clearing app data or uninstalling the App. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their own policies. Since we do not run user accounts or store your data on our servers, there is no account data for us to delete.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data.
